Frequently asked questions.

The quick answers to what people usually ask before trying FOB.

Is Future Operating Base really free?

Yes — free to use today, and it's staying that way. FOB is funded through grants and strategic investment, never by charging the veterans using it.

Is this affiliated with the VA?

No. Future Operating Base is an independent resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. It's built to help you navigate the system that exists, not to replace or speak for it.

Do I need to create an account to use it?

No — you can browse Future Operating Base without signing in. Creating an account (via a passwordless email link) lets your progress sync across devices, but it's optional.

When does the official app launch?

The beta is live now. Official App Store and Google Play launch is planned for November 10, 2026.

What does FOB actually do?

It turns military benefits, VA claims, and your separation timeline into plain English — a personalized path based on your real EAS date and branch, covering education, housing, documents, and VA claims info in one place.

Is the information accurate?

Every source is a real regulation or official benefit, translated out of bureaucratic language — not simplified into something inaccurate. See our Resources page for sourced explainers on specific VA rules.

I have a question about my specific VA claim — can FOB help?

FOB is built to help you understand the system, not to handle individual case status. For official case questions, contact va.gov directly.

How do I get in touch?

Email info@futureoperatingbase.com, or visit the Contact page for other ways to reach us.
